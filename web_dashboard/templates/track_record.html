How to read this page

Every directional stance the AI takes (BUY, SELL, BULLISH, BEARISH, AVOID, …) is recorded. 30 days later the stock's price change is compared to the Russell 2000 index (^RUT) over the same window.

  • Excess return = the stock's % return minus the index's % return, in percentage points (pp). Positive means the stock beat the market.
  • Hit = the call was directionally right vs the market: a bullish call on a stock that beat the index, or a bearish call on one that lagged it.
  • Hit rate = hits ÷ scored calls. Because excess return is roughly a coin flip by construction, ~50% means no edge. What makes money is a positive mean excess return — being very right on hits and only slightly wrong on misses.
  • Treat groups with fewer than ~30 scored calls as noise, not signal.
