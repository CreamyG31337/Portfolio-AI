## Sector Frameworks Cheat Sheet

Use sector-native metrics before general valuation shortcuts.

### Tech/SaaS
- Focus on ARR/MRR quality, retention, Rule of 40, and burn runway.

### Mining/Resources
- Evaluate grade, AISC, jurisdiction risk, and NI 43-101 reliability.

### REITs
- Prefer FFO/AFFO and NAV framing over EPS-centric interpretation.

### Financials
- Track book value trend, NIM dynamics, credit quality, and loan growth.

### Cannabis/Energy
- Cannabis: MSO vs LP regulatory exposure and execution risk.
- Energy: reserves quality, decline rates, hedge profile, and break-even pricing.

### Output Requirements
- Mention the sector-specific metric set used in the final reasoning.
