## 2025-02-24 - Faster Supabase client DataFrame iterations
**Learning:** Found an anti-pattern in `web_dashboard/supabase_client.py` where pandas `df.iterrows()` was used extensively to construct dictionaries for batch insertion/updating in PostgREST and formatting data. `iterrows()` builds a `Series` for every row, yielding large overheads (~1.12 seconds for 10k rows in local benchmarking).
**Action:** Used `df.to_dict('records')` upfront instead of `df.iterrows()`. This pushes row-wise extraction entirely into C/Cython and creates standard python dictionaries quickly (~0.2 seconds for 10k rows, roughly 5-10x faster). Because this operates identically to unpacking `_, row in ...` in terms of dictionary column access (e.g. `row["Ticker"]`), the refactor was drop-in and perfectly safe.
